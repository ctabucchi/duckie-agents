- Reliability pattern, not one-offs: license/license-drop, trigger, and Slack-integration failures have each recurred within the same two-week window.
- Key-person risk with Joel gone — no clear replacement with the same context yet.
- 60%-goal blockers (ISGI, tapechart) have zero movement despite being flagged repeatedly to stakeholders.
- QA/reporting gaps: Desiree's QA dashboard ask and missing Jul 12 evaluations are still open — this was also Decagon's stated strength.

## Recommendation to bring to Ashrit

Renew short-term (not annual), same logic as the April call: lock in through the next few months rather than a long commitment, conditioned on:

1. Committed timeline on ISGI + tapechart (the two blockers to 60%) — not just "in progress."
2. A named primary Duckie contact replacing Joel, with the same access/context.
3. Credit/cost model resolved for in-stay triage before we turn it on at scale.
4. QA dashboard + reporting fixed — our current blind spot and Decagon's strongest pitch point.

If Duckie won't commit to those, Decagon is a credible fallback — but re-opening that eval means resetting months of integration work, so it shouldn't be the default.
